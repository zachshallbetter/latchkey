Deliverables

What the engagement leaves you with

A result, not a license: your team's credentials organized, its Macs set up, and the procedures written down.

  • Credential and environment inventoryEvery env file under your projects, where each value would go, and what's shared, skipped or in conflict.
  • Dependency and migration reportWhich projects and environments use which credentials, the duplicates and stale ones, and the order to move them in.
  • Latchkey set up on supported MacsProjects linked to their repositories, env files of references, and environment records your developers can export on any Mac.
  • Access and recovery proceduresHow each developer gets a credential, what agents may read without asking, how grants are made, and how to rotate and restore.
  • Onboarding and handoffA walkthrough for your developers, and the documentation to bring the next one on without us.
  • Safer agent accessGrants scoped to folders, sessions and commands, and the agent instructions that keep values out of transcripts.

How it runs

  1. 1Agree scopeWhich projects, Macs and deployment platforms, and how credentials are handled, in writing.
  2. 2InventoryDry runs on your team's Macs: names, counts and conflicts, never values.
  3. 3MigrateImports with one approval per developer, references in place of values, projects and records set up.
  4. 4Hand offProcedures, onboarding, and a check that every project runs through Latchkey.

How your credentials are handled

  • Values stay on your team's Macs. Latchkey stores them in each developer's iCloud Keychain; the inventory and reports work from names and counts, which is all lk import --dry-run prints.
  • Each import and grant is approved by the developer whose Mac it is, with Touch ID.
  • Scope, confidentiality and credential-handling terms are agreed in writing before we start. We don't accept customer secrets outside those terms.

What it covers, and what it doesn't

Fixed scope, limited to what Latchkey supports today.

  • Covered: Macs on macOS 15 or later, env files and the local workflows Latchkey supports, and linking environments to Vercel, GitHub Actions, Railway or Fly.
  • Not covered: Windows or Linux machines, production secrets platforms we'd have to replace, or a security audit. It's an implementation engagement, not security consulting.
  • Pricing starts at $2,500 for a small team; larger scopes are quoted before the work is agreed.

Latchkey is for one person's Macs today. Teams would add what organizations automating development ask for:
