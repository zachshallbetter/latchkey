Questions

Good questions, short answers

Is Latchkey free?

Yes. Everything Latchkey does today is free to download and use, with no account or trial. Storage, references, import, running commands, approvals, the security protections and export stay free when Pro arrives.

What will Pro add?

Pro is the premium Mac tool for one developer working across projects, Macs and agents: agent controls, recovery and migration, rotation and lifecycle, and provider integrations. They're planned, and none is sold before it exists. Shared team environments are Latchkey Teams, a separate product.

Can I buy Pro now?

No. Pro isn't on sale. It will be a one-time in-app purchase in the Mac App Store version, with its features stated before anyone pays. Ask to hear when it is.

Will the free download stay?

Yes. The direct download stays free, with everything that is free in Latchkey. Pro is only in the Mac App Store version.

Will my credentials ever depend on a license?

No. Your values stay in your iCloud Keychain, and you can always read or export them through Latchkey's approvals, whatever plan you're on.

Does Latchkey send my credentials anywhere?

Not to Latchkey: there's no Latchkey server or account. Values sync through iCloud Keychain to your own Macs, and leave them only where you send them: to a command you've allowed, or to a deployment platform you push to.

Is the source available?

Latchkey will be source-available under the Functional Source License: free to read, change and use for anything but a competing product, with each version becoming Apache 2.0 two years after release. Read it on GitHub.

When is Teams coming?

When real teams have shown they'll pay for it. We're looking for two or three design partners first; nothing is built before then.

Can you help my team move over?

Yes. The Developer Credential Migration is a fixed-scope engagement for small teams on Macs, from $2,500. See what it includes.

How do refunds and other Macs work?

Apple handles both: refunds through reportaproblem.apple.com, and Pro unlocks on the Macs signed in to your Apple Account. See the terms.
