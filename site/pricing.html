Questions

Good questions, short answers

Is Latchkey free?

Yes. During the development preview everything it does is free. Storage, references, import, running commands, approvals, the security protections and export stay free when Pro arrives.

What will Pro add?

Tools that save time for people with many projects, Macs and agents: project recovery and migration, expiry and rotation help, agent workflow conveniences, and dependency analysis. They're planned, and none is sold before it exists.

Can I buy Pro now?

No. Pro isn't on sale. An early-adopter license at $59 will come first, with its features and update terms stated before anyone pays.

What happens when my year of updates ends?

The version you have keeps working. Pro Updates, $39 a year, brings new features for another year; skipping it doesn't turn anything off.

Will my credentials ever depend on a license?

No. Your values stay in your iCloud Keychain, and you can always read or export them through Latchkey's approvals, whatever plan you're on.

Does Latchkey send my credentials anywhere?

Not to Latchkey: there's no Latchkey server or account. Values sync through iCloud Keychain to your own Macs, and leave them only where you send them: to a command you've allowed, or to a deployment platform you push to.

Is the source available?

Latchkey will be source-available: its source published for anyone to read, but not open source. The license is still being chosen, and the repository isn't public yet.

When is Teams coming?

When real teams have shown they'll pay for it. We're looking for two or three design partners first; nothing is built before then.

Can you help my team move over?

Yes. The Developer Credential Migration is a fixed-scope engagement for small teams on Macs, from $2,500. See what it includes.

How do refunds and other Macs work?

Those terms will be published with Pro, before it goes on sale.
