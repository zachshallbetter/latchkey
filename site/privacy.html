Privacy

Privacy policy

Latchkey collects nothing about you. There is no Latchkey server, account, telemetry, analytics or advertising. Snack Studio never receives your credentials or anything about how you use them.

Effective 12 November 2026. Applies to the Latchkey app, its command-line tool, MCP server and Safari extension, and to this website.

What stays on your Macs

  • Your values are kept in your keychain: iCloud Keychain, which syncs them end to end encrypted between your own Apple devices, or this Mac's login keychain. Apple can't read iCloud Keychain contents, and neither can we.
  • Item details, environment records, grants and the access log are kept in your keychain and in Latchkey's folder on your Mac. They never leave it except through iCloud Keychain sync or an export you ask for.
  • Folders and files you choose are remembered on your Mac so Latchkey can rewrite them later. Removing the permission forgets them.

When values leave your Mac

Only when you, or a command you allowed, sends them:

  • Commands you run with lk run receive the values you approved, and do with them what they do.
  • Deployment platforms you push to (Vercel, GitHub, Railway, Fly) receive the values you push, either through their own command-line tools and your own sign-in to them, or, in the Mac App Store version and for any link where you choose a token item, in direct requests from the app to the service’s own API (api.vercel.com, api.github.com, backboard.railway.com, api.machines.dev) using the access token in the item you chose, which is sent only to that service. Their privacy policies apply.
  • The key proxy forwards your requests to OpenAI or Anthropic with your key, when you run a command with --proxy.
  • Short-lived AWS credentials are requested from AWS (sts.amazonaws.com) with your key, when you ask for them.
  • The Safari extension fills or saves values on the pages you choose, after your approval.
  • The clipboard: copied values stay on this Mac, are hidden from clipboard history apps, and are cleared after a short time.

Updates

The copy of Latchkey downloaded from this site checks https://latchkey.snackstud.io/appcast.xml for new versions. That's a standard web request with Latchkey's version number; no system profile (hardware, operating system or usage details) is sent. Turn it off in Settings with Check for updates automatically, or check by hand with Check for Updates…. The Mac App Store version is updated by the App Store.

Purchases

Latchkey Pro will be sold by Apple through the Mac App Store; it isn't on sale yet. Apple processes the payment and tells the app, on your Mac, that Pro is unlocked; we receive Apple's standard sales reports, which don't identify you. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Crash reports

If you share crash reports and analytics with app developers in macOS settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports for Latchkey. They never contain your values.

This website

The site uses no cookies, analytics or third-party requests: fonts are your system's, and every script is served from this site. The host keeps standard request logs (address, page, time) for operating the site.

Email

If you write to latchkey@snackstud.io, we keep the conversation to answer you and delete it when it's no longer needed. Never send a real secret.

Children

Latchkey is a developer tool and isn't directed at children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date. Latchkey is made by Snack Studio. Questions: latchkey@snackstud.io.
